Chapter 01 · The Idea

Paris,
Île de
la Cité.

Alexandra discovers the idea. It begins here, on the banks of the Seine. Alexandra spots a basket — colourful, handmade, imperfect. She sees something else entirely: a canvas. A story waiting to be embroidered. The coordinates are set.

48°51'11"N · 2°20'59"E — The starting point of every basket.

The
Drawing

Before a single thread is woven, the basket lives on a screen.

Artists imagine every embroidery, every colour, every motif. The illustration is a blueprint — a dream translated into instructions for hands that will never meet the person who drew it.

Lola, Cecilia, Sarah, Charlotte
and many others create the illustrations.

Chapter 03 · The Craft

Essaouira

Said orchestrates his family — Hafsa, Aicha and Imane.

The metal structure — what they call a "gabarit" — and the palm leaves are woven in the Atlas Mountains. Said gives the final eye for the quality control.

No machine. No shortcut.
Just hands and time.

74 Artisans

Hassna, Sabah, Roquaia, Nezha, Ibtisam, Latifa — and many more hands.

3 Cities

Said buys threads in Essaouira, Casablanca and Marrakech.

Chapter 04 · The Weaving

The base is knitted, the background crocheted, the relief embroidered.

Said drives his car full of basket-candies from Essaouira to Marrakech.

Chapter 05 · The Crossing

Tanger
→
Algeciras

We have a truck that leaves from Marrakech to Tanger, Algeciras — last stop: Clichy.

Fourteen kilometres of sea between two continents. From there, 2,500 kilometres north through Spain and France.

~2,500 km by road.
One ferry. One truck.
Two continents.
