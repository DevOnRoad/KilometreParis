Chapter 03 · The Craft

Essaouira

Said orchestrates his family — Hafsa, Aicha and Imane.

The metal structure — what they call a "gabarit" — and the palm leaves are woven in the Atlas Mountains. Said's family checks every basket by hand. The quality control happens on a plastic stool, under palm trees.

Chapter 04 · The Weaving

The
Hands

Hassna, Sabah, Roquaia, Nezha, Ibtisam, Latifa — and many more.

Said buys the threads in Essaouira, Casablanca and Marrakech, searching for the right colours. Each basket changes hands a dozen times before it is finished. The base is knitted, the background crocheted, the relief embroidered.

Chapter 05 · The Crossing

Tanger
→
Algeciras

We have a truck that leaves from Marrakech to Tanger, Algeciras — last stop: Clichy.

The Mediterranean crossing. Fourteen kilometres of sea between two continents. The truck boards the Maroc Express ferry at Tanger and arrives in Algeciras, Spain. From there, 2,500 kilometres north.

~2,500 km by road.
One ferry. One truck.
Two continents.

Chapter 06 · The Arrival

Clichy
→ Vitry

Hassan grabs the treasures in Clichy to deliver them in Vitry-sur-Seine.

The baskets arrive in the suburbs of Paris. Hassan unloads in Clichy, drives south to Vitry-sur-Seine. The trip is not finished — immediately they set out by train from Gare Montparnasse to Orgerus.

Chapter 07 · The Shoot

Orgerus

The pieces are photographed by Alexandra, Thomas and Côme in "La Roseraie" of the Castle of Courgent.

A TER train from Montparnasse. A castle in the countryside. A rose garden. Alexandra holds the basket — the same one that started in Morocco, crossed the sea, travelled by truck and train. Now it faces a camera. Almost ready.
