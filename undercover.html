Chapter 01 · The Idea

Paris

Paris, Île de la Cité.
Alexandra discovers the idea.

It begins here, on the banks of the Seine. Alexandra spots a basket — colourful, handmade, imperfect. She sees something else: a canvas. A story waiting to be embroidered. She writes the first sketch on a napkin. The coordinates are set.

48°51'11"N 2°20'59"E

Chapter 02 · The Vision

The Drawing

Lola, Cecilia, Sarah, Charlotte and many others, create the illustrations.

Before a single thread is woven, the basket lives on a screen. Artists imagine every embroidery, every colour, every motif. The illustration is a blueprint — a dream translated into instructions for hands that will never meet the person who drew it.

Every design begins
as a thought bubble.

Chapter 03 · The Craft

Essaouira

Said orchestrates his family — Hafsa, Aicha and Imane. Said gives the final eye for the quality control.

The metal structure — what they call a "gabarit" — and the palm leaves are woven in the Atlas Mountains. Said's family checks every basket by hand. The quality control happens on a plastic stool, under palm trees, with salt air from the port.

No machine. No shortcut.
Just hands and time.

Chapter 04 · The Weaving

The Hands

The base and background are knitted by Hassna, Sabah, Roquaia and many other women. Nezha, Ibtisam and Latifa bring the imaginery comes to life by crocheting the "relief".

Said buys the threads in Essaouira, Casablanca and Marrakech, searching for the right colours. Each basket changes hands a dozen times before it is finished. Said drives his car full of basket-candies from Essaouira to Marrakech.

Said B. Essaouira
Marrakech — our 74 artisans.

Chapter 05 · The Crossing

Tanger →
Algeciras

We have a truck that leaves from Marrakech to Tanger, Algeciras and last stop: Clichy.

The Mediterranean crossing. Fourteen kilometres of sea between two continents. The truck boards the Maroc Express ferry at Tanger, and arrives in Algeciras, Spain. From there, the road north begins — through Spain, across France, all the way to the suburbs of Paris.

~2,500 km by road.
One ferry. One truck.

Chapter 06 · The Arrival

Clichy →
Vitry

Hassan grabs the treasures in Clichy to deliver us in Vitry-sur-Seine. The trip is not finished — immediately they set out by train from Gare Montparnasse to Orgerus.

The baskets arrive in the Paris suburbs. Hassan unloads them in Clichy. They travel south to Vitry-sur-Seine. Then by train from Gare Montparnasse. The streets of Paris carry them before you do.

Chapter 07 · The Shoot

Orgerus

The pieces are photographed by Alexandra, Thomas and their little assistant Côme in "La Roseraie" of the Castle of Courgent.

The TER from Montparnasse. A castle in the countryside. A rose garden. Alexandra holds the basket — the same one that started in Morocco, crossed the sea, travelled by truck and train. Now it is in front of a camera. Almost ready.

Gare Montparnasse → Orgerus
One hour by train.

Chapter 08 · The Destination

The World

The basket is ready to fly to you, shops, beaches, mountains and cities all over the world.

The trip is not finished, and barely twenty of the kilometres have been started. This basket will travel further than most people ever will. It will go to places Said has never been. It will carry your things, your memories, your life.

This is not just a basket.
This is a Kilometre.
